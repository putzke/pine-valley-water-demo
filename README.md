# Pine Valley Water Supply Project: Concept Demonstration

A static website that shows how a public involvement team (Sunrise Engineering with Black & Veatch)
would keep the community informed during design and construction of the Pine Valley Water Supply Project.

**This is a concept demonstration. It is not the official website of the Central Iron County Water Conservancy District.**
Items marked "Sample" or "Illustrative" (monitoring readings, cost checkpoint, construction notice, camera, hotline,
forms) are placeholders. Facts come from public District, BLM and project documents and should be confirmed
with the District before any public use.

## Files
- `index.html`: all page content
- `styles.css`: design
- `app.js`: English/Spanish toggle, crossing tabs, sample monitoring chart, searchable FAQ, demo forms

## Publish with GitHub Pages
Settings > Pages > Build and deployment > Source: "Deploy from a branch" > Branch: `main` / `(root)` > Save.
